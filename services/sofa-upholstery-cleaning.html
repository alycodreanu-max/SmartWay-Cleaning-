About This Service

Why professional upholstery cleaning matters

Sofas, chairs and upholstered furniture are used every day. Over time, fabric can collect dust, crumbs, skin particles, body oils, pet hair, food residue and accidental spills.

Routine vacuuming helps with surface debris, but it cannot always reach residue held deeper within suitable fabric fibres and cushions.

Smart Way Cleaning starts with a fabric, care-label and condition check. We then select an appropriate method for the item, which may include professional vacuuming, stain pre-treatment and controlled extraction cleaning.

The aim is to improve appearance, freshness and cleanliness while protecting the material and setting honest expectations about the final result.

Important: upholstery cleaning is not furniture restoration. Permanent dye loss, sun fading, bleach marks, burns, worn fibres, fabric damage and some older stains may remain visible after cleaning.

Common Questions

Sofa and upholstery cleaning FAQs

How long does upholstery cleaning take?

Many standard sofas take approximately 1 to 3 hours, depending on size, fabric, condition, stains, cushion count and selected extras.

How long will the furniture take to dry?

Many suitable fabrics dry in approximately 4 to 8 hours. Material, filling, humidity, room temperature, ventilation and treatment level all affect drying time.

Can you clean a leather sofa?

Leather requires specialist leather cleaning and conditioning rather than standard fabric extraction. Send photos and the care label before booking so we can confirm whether the item is suitable.

Will cleaning damage my sofa fabric?

We assess the care label, fabric, colour stability and condition before choosing a method. Delicate, aged, previously damaged or incorrectly treated fabrics may carry additional risk.

Can you remove pet hair and odour?

Pet hair and odours can often be reduced significantly. Complete removal cannot always be guaranteed where contamination has reached deep into cushions or internal filling.

Will every stain disappear?

No. Results depend on stain type, age, fabric, previous products used and whether permanent dye or fibre damage has already occurred.

Is fabric protection worth adding?

Fabric protection may help suitable materials resist future spills and make routine maintenance easier. It does not make furniture completely stain-proof.

Can I use the sofa the same day?

Only after the fabric and cushions are fully dry. Minimise use until the item is dry and follow the ventilation advice provided.
