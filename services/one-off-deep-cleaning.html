About This Service

Deep cleaning that makes a visible difference

A one-off deep clean goes beyond routine cleaning. It gives more attention to the areas where dust, grease, limescale and everyday build-up collect over time.

Our team works through the agreed rooms and priority areas, including kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living spaces, hallways, doors, skirting boards, fittings and floors.

This service is suitable for seasonal resets, pre-sale property presentation, moving preparation, special occasions, busy households or homes that have not received a detailed clean for some time.

There is no recurring commitment. You book the service once, agree the scope and choose any specialist add-ons needed.

The final scope and price depend on the property size, condition, access, location and selected extras.

Common Questions

One-off deep cleaning FAQs

What is the difference between regular cleaning and deep cleaning?

Regular cleaning focuses on routine upkeep. A deep clean gives more time and attention to built-up dirt, skirting boards, doors, fittings, cupboard areas, kitchens, bathrooms and other detailed surfaces.

How long does a deep clean take?

Many standard deep cleans take around 4 to 8 hours, depending on property size, condition, team size, priority areas and selected extras. Larger or heavily soiled properties may take longer.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. You can arrange access through a lockbox, neighbour, agent or another agreed method.

Can I add carpet or oven cleaning?

Yes. Professional carpet extraction, dip-tank oven cleaning, appliance cleaning, upholstery cleaning and mattress cleaning can be added.

Do you cover my area?

We cover the Smart Way Cleaning locations listed on our Areas page across parts of London, Essex, Hertfordshire and Cambridge.

Is there a satisfaction guarantee?

If an included item is missed, contact us within 48 hours. We will review the issue and arrange a correction where it falls within the agreed scope.

Do I need to provide cleaning products or equipment?

No. Our cleaners bring the standard professional products, equipment and tools required for the agreed service. Eco-friendly products are available on request.

Is a contract required?

No. This is a one-off service with no recurring cleaning commitment.
