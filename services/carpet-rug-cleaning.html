About This Service

Carpet cleaning that reaches deeper than routine vacuuming

Carpets collect dust, soil, hair, spills and everyday residue over time. A household vacuum helps with loose surface dirt, but it cannot always reach what has settled deeper into the carpet fibres.

Our professional carpet cleaning service starts with an inspection of the carpet type, condition, stains and access. Suitable areas are pre-treated before professional extraction cleaning is used to loosen and remove more of the built-up dirt.

This service is suitable for homes, rental properties, move-in preparation, end of tenancy cleaning, households with pets and rooms that need a visible refresh.

Most carpets are left damp rather than soaking wet. Drying time depends on carpet fibre, room temperature, humidity, ventilation and weather.

Important: no responsible carpet cleaner can promise complete removal of every stain. Results depend on the carpet material, stain type, age, previous treatments and permanent fibre or colour damage.

Common Questions

Carpet cleaning FAQs

How long will my carpets take to dry?

Many carpets dry in approximately 2 to 6 hours. The exact time depends on fibre type, room temperature, humidity, ventilation, weather and how heavily the carpet required cleaning.

Can you remove pet stains and odours?

Many pet-related stains and odours can improve significantly. Complete removal is not always possible where urine has reached the backing, underlay or flooring below, or where the fibre has been permanently damaged.

Is extraction cleaning safe for all carpets?

No single method is suitable for every carpet. We assess fibre type, backing, colour stability and condition before selecting the cleaning approach.

Do I need to move furniture?

Small movable items should ideally be removed before arrival. Larger or fragile furniture should be discussed when requesting the quote. Some items may need to remain in place and be cleaned around.

Do you cover my area?

We cover the Smart Way Cleaning locations listed on our Areas page across parts of London, Essex, Hertfordshire and Cambridge.

How often should carpets be professionally cleaned?

Many homes choose professional cleaning every 12 to 18 months. Homes with children, pets, allergies or heavy foot traffic may benefit from more frequent cleaning.

Are your products safe for children and pets?

Suitable household-conscious products can be selected. Tell us about children, pets, allergies or sensitivities before the booking so the correct approach can be discussed.

Will every stain disappear?

No. Stain results depend on the substance, carpet fibre, age of the stain, previous products used and whether permanent colour or fibre damage has occurred.

Can I walk on the carpet after cleaning?

Light walking may be possible with clean footwear or suitable shoe covers, but it is best to minimise traffic until the carpet is fully dry.
