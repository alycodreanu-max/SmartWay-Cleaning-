About This Service

End of tenancy cleaning built around the final inspection

Moving out can be stressful. You may be packing, changing your address, arranging keys and trying to leave the property in the condition expected by your landlord or letting agent.

Our end of tenancy cleaning service is designed to make that part of your move easier. We clean the property room by room, covering the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living spaces, hallways and agreed appliances.

The service includes cupboards, bathroom limescale attention, internal windows where safely accessible, carpet vacuuming and hard-floor cleaning.Oven cleaning, and fridge can be added separately.

Professional carpet extraction, upholstery cleaning and specialist appliance treatments can be added separately.

Important: no cleaning company can control the final deposit decision made by a landlord, letting agent or tenancy deposit scheme. Our service is designed to give you the best possible chance of leaving the property inspection-ready.

Common Questions

End of tenancy cleaning FAQs

What does end of tenancy cleaning include?

Our standard service covers the kitchen, oven, hob, empty fridge, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, hallways, cupboards, skirting boards, doors, switches, internal windows where safely accessible, carpet vacuuming and hard-floor cleaning. The final included scope is confirmed before booking.

Is your end of tenancy cleaning guaranteed?

We cannot guarantee the return of your deposit because the final decision belongs to the landlord, letting agent or tenancy deposit scheme. If an included item is flagged, contact us within 48 hours and we will review it and arrange a free correction where it falls within the agreed scope.

How much does end of tenancy cleaning cost?

Prices start from £155 for a studio flat. The final price depends on the property size, condition, number of bathrooms, access, location and selected extras. The full quote is confirmed before the booking.

Do I need to provide cleaning equipment or products?

No. Our cleaners arrive with the professional equipment and products required for the agreed service. Eco-friendly products are available on request.

Do you offer end of tenancy cleaning nationwide?

No. We currently cover the Smart Way Cleaning areas listed on our Areas page across parts of London, Essex, Hertfordshire and Cambridge.

How quickly can you clean my property?

Many standard properties take around 3 to 6 hours, depending on size, condition, team size and selected extras. Larger or heavily soiled properties may take longer.

Is carpet cleaning included in end of tenancy cleaning?

Carpet vacuuming is included. Professional carpet extraction cleaning is an optional add-on and should be selected when requesting your quote.

Do I need to be present during the clean?

No. You only need to arrange safe access. Customers may use a lockbox, leave a key with a neighbour or arrange access through a letting agent.

Is a deposit required to book?

No standard booking deposit is required. Payment is normally made after the service, unless different written terms are agreed for a large or specialist project.
