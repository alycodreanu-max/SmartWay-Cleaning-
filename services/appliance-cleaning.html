About This Service

Professional cleaning for hard-working household appliances

Kitchen and household appliances work every day. Over time, food residue, grease, crumbs, limescale, soap deposits and trapped moisture can build up inside and around them.

This build-up can create unpleasant smells, affect hygiene and make appliances harder to keep clean through routine wiping.

Smart Way Cleaning provides detailed cleaning for suitable accessible parts of ovens, fridges, freezers, dishwashers, washing machines, microwaves and selected small appliances.

You can book a single appliance or combine several appliances into a tailored kitchen package. The exact method depends on the appliance, material, condition and manufacturer access design.

Appliance cleaning is not appliance repair. We do not dismantle sealed electrical systems, repair faults or guarantee improved mechanical performance. Any fault, leak or electrical concern should be checked by a qualified engineer.

Common Questions

Appliance cleaning FAQs

Can I use appliances straight after cleaning?

Many appliances can be used shortly after cleaning, but drying, ventilation and safe restart times vary by appliance and treatment. We provide guidance after the service.

Do I need to empty my fridge first?

Yes. Fridges should normally be empty before arrival so shelves, drawers, walls and the base can be cleaned properly.

Does my freezer need to be defrosted?

Yes. The freezer should be empty and fully defrosted before the appointment unless another arrangement has been agreed.

Are your products food-safe?

Suitable food-safe products are used where appropriate for food storage and preparation surfaces.

What does the full kitchen package include?

The package can include a tailored combination of oven, hob, extractor, microwave, fridge, freezer, dishwasher and washing machine cleaning.

Can appliance cleaning be added to end of tenancy cleaning?

Yes. Appliance cleaning is commonly added to end of tenancy, move-in and one-off deep cleaning services.

Do you repair appliances?

No. We provide cleaning only. Mechanical faults, leaks, electrical faults and error messages should be checked by a qualified appliance engineer.

Do you cover my area?

We cover the Smart Way Cleaning locations listed on our Areas page across parts of London, Essex, Hertfordshire and Cambridge.

Can I book several appliances together?

Yes. Multi-appliance packages are available and may offer better value than separate bookings.
