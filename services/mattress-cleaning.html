About This Service

Why professional mattress cleaning can make a real difference

Mattresses are used for many hours every night. Over time they can collect dust, dead skin cells, sweat, pet hair, body oils, spills and other everyday residue.

Regular vacuuming and mattress protectors help with routine care, but they may not remove residue that has settled deeper into suitable surface fibres.

Smart Way Cleaning begins with a material and condition assessment. Suitable mattresses may then receive professional vacuuming, stain pre-treatment and extraction cleaning using a controlled amount of moisture.

The aim is to improve cleanliness, freshness and the visible condition of the mattress while protecting the fabric and internal construction.

Important: mattress cleaning is not a medical treatment. We do not promise to cure allergies, asthma or other health conditions. Stain, odour and allergen reduction depend on the mattress material, condition, age and depth of contamination.

Common Questions

Mattress cleaning FAQs

How long will my mattress take to dry?

Many suitable mattresses dry in approximately 4 to 8 hours, but drying time can vary. Material, filling, room temperature, ventilation, humidity and the amount of treatment required all affect the result. Do not replace bedding until the mattress is fully dry.

How often should I have my mattress professionally cleaned?

Many households consider professional mattress cleaning every 12 to 18 months. Homes with pets, children, spills or heavier use may choose more frequent cleaning.

Can you remove urine stains?

Urine stains and odours can often improve, but complete removal cannot be guaranteed. Results depend on the age of the stain, the mattress material and how deeply the liquid has reached the filling.

Is extraction cleaning safe for memory foam?

Not every cleaning method is suitable for every memory foam mattress. We assess the care label, construction and condition before deciding whether controlled extraction or another method is appropriate.

Do you clean both sides of the mattress?

Both sides can be cleaned when requested, suitable and safely accessible. Additional time and cost may apply.

Will mattress cleaning remove dust mites and allergens?

Professional vacuuming and suitable cleaning may reduce dust, residue and some allergens. We do not claim complete elimination and do not provide medical treatment or health guarantees.

Will every stain disappear?

No. Results depend on the stain, mattress material, age, previous products used and permanent colour or fibre damage.

Do you cover my area?

We cover the Smart Way Cleaning locations listed on our Areas page across parts of London, Essex, Hertfordshire and Cambridge.

Can I sleep on the mattress the same night?

Only if the mattress is completely dry. We recommend arranging the appointment early and allowing plenty of ventilation and drying time.
