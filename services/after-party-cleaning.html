About This Service

Professional cleaning after parties, celebrations and events

A successful party can leave behind bottles, cans, food packaging, sticky floors, used bathrooms, rearranged furniture, confetti and accidental spills.

Smart Way Cleaning helps restore the agreed areas without leaving you to spend the next day sorting through the mess. We work through kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, hallways, floors and other selected spaces.

Suitable everyday party waste can be collected and bagged. Carpets, rugs, sofas and fabric chairs can be assessed separately where drinks, food or other marks need specialist treatment.

The service can be used after house parties, children’s birthdays, family events, garden parties, office celebrations and suitable rented venues.

Important: waste bagging does not automatically include transport, disposal fees, hazardous waste, broken glass in unsafe conditions, large-volume commercial waste or items requiring licensed disposal. Tell us exactly what remains when requesting your quote.

Common Questions

After party cleaning FAQs

How quickly can you clean after a party?

Same-day and next-morning requests may be available depending on your location, the size of the clean and team availability. Contact us as early as possible for urgent requests.

Do you remove rubbish and bottles?

We can collect and bag suitable everyday party waste, including bottles, cans, food packaging and decorations, within the agreed scope. Transport, disposal charges and specialist waste are not automatically included.

Can you treat carpet and sofa stains?

Yes. Suitable carpets and upholstery can be assessed for specialist treatment. Complete stain removal cannot be guaranteed because the result depends on the material, stain type, age and permanent damage.

Do you clean gardens and outdoor areas?

Suitable patios, garden seating areas and everyday outdoor event debris can be assessed. Large waste volumes or specialist disposal may require a separate service.

Can you clean a rented venue?

Yes, suitable rented venues and function rooms can be assessed. Please provide the venue size, access time, facilities and required completion deadline.

Do you cover my area?

We cover the Smart Way Cleaning locations listed on our Areas page across parts of London, Essex, Hertfordshire and Cambridge.

Do I need to provide cleaning products or equipment?

No. Our cleaners bring the standard professional products, tools and equipment needed for the agreed service.

Do I need to be at the property?

No. Safe access can be arranged through a lockbox, neighbour, venue representative or another agreed method.

Is washing dishes included?

A small or moderate amount can be included when agreed before the booking. Large quantities, catering equipment or specialist glassware may affect the quote.
