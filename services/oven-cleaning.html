About This Service

Professional oven cleaning across Smart Way service areas

A dirty oven is more than an unpleasant sight. Burnt-on grease, carbon deposits and old food residue can cause smoke, smells and poor cooking results.

Our professional oven cleaning service uses a specialist dip-tank method for suitable removable components. Racks, trays, removable panels and other safe-to-remove parts are treated separately, hand-finished and carefully reassembled.

We use food-safe, biodegradable and non-caustic cleaning products. There are no harsh fumes and no strong chemical smell left in your kitchen.

Common Questions

Oven cleaning FAQs

How long does oven cleaning take?

A standard single or double oven usually takes around 1.5 to 3 hours. Range cookers, Aga and heavily soiled ovens may take longer.

Can I use my oven straight after cleaning?

In most cases, the oven can be used around 30 minutes after the cleaning is complete.

Are your products safe around food?

Yes. We use food-safe, non-caustic products suitable for oven and appliance cleaning.

Do you clean Aga and range cookers?

Yes. We clean range cookers, Aga and Rayburn appliances. Please provide the model and photos when requesting a quote.

Do you cover my area?

We currently cover the Smart Way service areas listed on our Areas page.

Can you include the hob and extractor?

Yes. Hobs, extractor fans, microwaves and other appliances can be added to the booking.
